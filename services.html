Workshop servicing

Our workshop

Second to none

At Morley Mower Centre, our workshop servicing is second to none, ensuring your power equipment operates at peak performance throughout the seasons. Our expert technicians meticulously inspect and diagnose any issues, offering comprehensive repair and maintenance services tailored to your mower's needs.

From routine tune-ups to specialised blade sharpening, we handle it all with precision and care. We prioritise quality craftsmanship and use only genuine parts to guarantee lasting reliability and efficiency. Trust us to keep your mower running smoothly, so you can enjoy a perfectly manicured garden every time.

What we do

Our services

Engine repair

  • Rebuild or replacement
  • Carburettor adjustment or replacement
  • Engine tune-up (cleaning, oil change, etc.)
  • Compression testing and adjustment

Blade & chain maintenance

  • Blade sharpening (including specialised reel mower sharpening)
  • Chain sharpening and tension adjustment
  • Chain replacement

Fuel system service

  • Fuel filter replacement
  • Fuel line inspection and replacement
  • Cleaning or replacing the air filter

Electrical system service

  • Battery testing and replacement (for electric models)
  • Wiring inspection and repair

Lubrication & greasing

  • Lubricating moving parts (bearings, joints, etc.)
  • Greasing gears and shafts

Transmission & gearbox repair

  • Gearbox oil change
  • Gear inspection and repair

Handle & grip replacement

  • Handlebar grip replacement
  • Handle repair or reinforcement

Safety check & adjustment

  • Brake inspection and adjustment
  • Safety switch inspection and repair

Accessory installation

  • Installing accessories like baggers and mulchers

General maintenance

  • Cleaning the mower or trimmer
  • Checking and tightening bolts and fasteners
  • Overall inspection for wear and tear

Troubleshooting & diagnostics

  • Identifying and fixing starting issues
  • Resolving performance problems (loss of power, uneven cutting, etc.)

Warranty service

  • Handling warranty claims and repairs

Specialised services

  • Specialised reel mower sharpening
  • Brush blade sharpening

Additional services

  • Blade balancing
  • Deck levelling (for mowers)

Consultation & advice

  • Advice on maintenance schedules and best practices
